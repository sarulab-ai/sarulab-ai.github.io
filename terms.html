Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-08-27

1. What this is

SARU LAB JARVIS ("JARVIS", "this tool") is an internal content-automation system built and operated by SARU LAB (「AI社員研究所」) for its own educational social media accounts. These Terms govern SARU LAB's own use of JARVIS, including its use of TikTok's API.

JARVIS is not a public product. It is not offered to, and does not accept, other users or accounts. It connects only to social media accounts that SARU LAB itself owns and has explicitly authorized through each platform's own login/authorization flow (including TikTok's official OAuth process).

2. What JARVIS does

  • Plans and drafts short educational scripts for SARU LAB's own content.
  • Produces narration and video for that content.
  • Runs an automated and human-reviewed quality check before anything is published.
  • Publishes the resulting content to SARU LAB's own authorized accounts on Instagram, YouTube, note, Threads, and (once this application is approved) TikTok.
  • Retrieves whatever performance metrics each platform's official API makes available for SARU LAB's own posts (for example: views, likes, comments, shares), and uses them to guide future content decisions.

3. Acceptable use

JARVIS is used only to publish SARU LAB's own educational content, in line with each connected platform's own developer terms and community guidelines (including TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Community Guidelines). It is not used to post spam, to impersonate anyone, or to access any account other than SARU LAB's own.

4. No warranty

JARVIS is provided as-is, as an internal tool. SARU LAB makes no warranty that any particular piece of content, publishing schedule, or metric will be available or accurate at all times — for example, a metric that a platform's API does not expose will not be available through JARVIS either.

5. Changes

These Terms may be updated as JARVIS's functionality changes (for example, as new platforms are connected). The "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly.
